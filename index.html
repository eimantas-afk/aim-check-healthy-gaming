01 / HYDRATION BUFF

DON'T
RUN ON EMPTY.

Your setup has cooling. Your body needs water.

FUN FACT: Even mild dehydration can make it harder to stay focused and feel comfortable.
💧 YOUR QUEST: Keep water within reach.
02 / 06
02 / SLEEP BUFF

YOUR BEST
UPGRADE?
SLEEP.

Grinding all night isn't always the winning play.

FUN FACT: Sleep supports memory and the learning of motor skills you've practiced.
🌙 YOUR QUEST: Aim for 7–9 hours.
03 / 06
03 / MOVEMENT BUFF

AFK
IS A POWER
MOVE.

Between matches, stand up and give your body a reset.

FUN FACT: Long periods in one position can contribute to stiffness and discomfort.
🏃 YOUR QUEST: Walk or stretch between games.
04 / 06
04 / EYE-CARE BUFF

DON'T GET
FLASHED
IRL.

Staring at a screen without breaks can tire your eyes.

FUN FACT: People often blink less while concentrating on screens, which can contribute to dryness.
👀 YOUR QUEST: Try the 20–20–20 rule.

Every 20 minutes, look ~20 feet away for 20 seconds.

05 / COMFORT BUFF + GG

FIX YOUR
SETUP.
NOT JUST
YOUR AIM.

Relax your shoulders, support your feet, and keep your mouse and keyboard comfortably within reach.

FINAL CHALLENGE: Water. Sleep. Movement. Eye breaks. Comfortable posture. That's your IRL loadout.
